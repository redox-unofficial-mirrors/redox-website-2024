Redox OS

Redox is a Unix-like Operating System written in Rust, aiming to bring the innovations of Rust to a modern microkernel and full set of applications.

Get Started

Why Redox

Redox is built on a Microkernel design, meaning it's highly stable by its very nature

Redox is implemented entirely in Rust - no more memory bugs!

Redox stands on the shoulders of giants, improving and building on the pioneering work of its predecessors

With inspiration drawn from Plan 9, Minix, seL4, BSD and Linux, Redox sees its architecture proven and battle-tested.

The entire Rust standard library is supported

Redox is Open Source, licensed under MIT

We have almost complete source-compatibility with Linux and BSD programs - your favourite software is only a build away!
